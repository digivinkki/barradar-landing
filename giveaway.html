BarRadar Rewards Giveaway Rules

Prize

One winner will receive unlimited BarRadar Rewards wheel spins for one evening at one selected participating bar.

How to enter

  1. Follow BarRadar Rewards on Instagram
  2. Like the giveaway post
  3. Comment on the giveaway post

Eligibility

Participants must be 18 years or older.

Prize conditions

The prize is personal and non-transferable. The prize is valid only during the agreed evening and at one selected participating bar.

The prize cannot be exchanged for cash or any other compensation. Rewards are limited to the prizes available in the BarRadar Rewards app for the selected bar.

Normal venue rules, age limits and responsible serving policies apply. The participating venue has the right to refuse service if necessary.

BarRadar Rewards reserves the right to disqualify entries or prize use in case of abuse, fraud, inappropriate behaviour or violation of the rules.

Winner selection

One winner will be selected randomly from eligible entries.

The winner will be contacted via Instagram direct message.

This giveaway is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by or associated with Instagram.
