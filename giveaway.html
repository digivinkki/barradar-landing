Bar Radar Rewards Giveaway Rules

Prize

One winner and one friend will receive a Bar Radar Rewards VIP night for one evening at one selected participating bar.

The giveaway recreates the Bar Radar Rewards wheel experience for one evening. The winner and one friend may personally redeem rewards during the agreed evening, subject to reasonable personal use.

How to enter

  1. Follow Bar Radar Rewards on Instagram
  2. Like the giveaway post
  3. Comment your favourite bar or tag a friend

Eligibility

Participants must be 18 years or older. The selected friend must also be 18 years or older.

Prize conditions

The prize is personal and non-transferable. It is valid only for the winner and one selected friend, during one agreed evening, at one selected participating bar.

The prize cannot be exchanged for cash or any other compensation. Rewards are limited to the type of rewards normally available through Bar Radar Rewards at the selected bar.

Rewards may only be redeemed one at a time and only by the winner and the selected friend personally. The prize does not include rewards or drinks for any other guests.

Normal venue rules, age limits and responsible serving policies apply. The participating venue has the right to refuse service if necessary.

Bar Radar Rewards reserves the right to disqualify entries or prize use in case of abuse, fraud, inappropriate behaviour or violation of the rules.

Winner selection

One winner will be selected randomly from eligible entries. The winner will be contacted via Instagram direct message.

Instagram disclaimer

This giveaway is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by or associated with Instagram.
